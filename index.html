Research

I study the structure of atomic nuclei and the role of isospin symmetry and its breaking in nuclear systems. My work connects microscopic nuclear-structure calculations with precision tests of fundamental physics.

Isospin Symmetry

Isospin-symmetry breaking, mirror asymmetries, isospin mixing, and charge-dependent nuclear interactions.

Superallowed β Decay

Isospin-symmetry-breaking corrections to superallowed Fermi transitions and their connection to precision tests of the weak interaction.

Nuclear Structure

Shell-model, ab initio, and nuclear density functional approaches to understanding the structure of atomic nuclei.

Current Research

Some of my current research topics include:

Isospin-symmetry breaking in superallowed β decay
Nuclear-structure corrections and their impact on precision determination of fundamental weak-interaction parameters.
Mirror asymmetry of isospin-symmetry-breaking corrections
Understanding the microscopic origin of mirror asymmetries in superallowed Fermi transitions.
E0 transitions and isospin mixing
Exploring correlations between E0 transition strengths and isospin-symmetry-breaking corrections.
